# Whiteboard 3 of 3 — Indian Examinations, Timelines, Admissions, Recruitment & Career Pathways

Whiteboard 1 mapped who has the authority to govern education, prescribe standards and conduct examinations.

Whiteboard 2 mapped which institutions provide education, which programmes they offer and what qualifications they award.

Whiteboard 3 will map the complete journey from eligibility to examination, selection, admission or appointment, qualification completion and the next career or education stage.

This will be the most process-intensive of the three boards. It must cover school examinations, engineering, medicine, science, commerce, finance, management, law, arts, defence, government recruitment, professional qualifications, research and other major Indian career pathways.

As with the earlier boards, this is the detailed content specification, not the creation of the actual whiteboard.

## The three-board system

Whiteboard 1 — Governance and authorities

Constitution, ministries, regulators, boards, examination agencies, legal powers and oversight.

Whiteboard 2 — Institutions and qualifications

Schools, universities, IITs, IIMs, ISB, AIIMS, programmes, qualifications and recognition.

Whiteboard 3 — Examinations and career pathways

Eligibility → application → examination → selection → admission or appointment → qualification → next opportunity.

We are specifying this board now

## 1. What this whiteboard must answer

For every examination, admission route, professional qualification or recruitment pathway, the board should answer:

1. Who is eligible to apply?
2. What educational qualification and subjects are required?
3. What age, nationality, attempt or experience requirements apply?
4. Which organisation conducts the examination?
5. Which organisation sets the eligibility criteria?
6. What are the application and examination timelines?
7. What is the examination format, syllabus and marking scheme?
8. Are there multiple stages, interviews, practical tests, physical tests or medical tests?
9. What does the result actually provide: a score, rank, eligibility certificate, admission opportunity or recruitment recommendation?
10. Who conducts counselling, allocates seats or makes the final appointment?
11. What qualification, training, licence or appointment follows?
12. What can the candidate do next if selected, not selected, ineligible or unsuccessful?
13. Which official notification governs the current cycle?
14. How does the pathway connect to Whiteboards 1 and 2?

Central principle: An examination is not the same thing as admission, and admission is not the same thing as completing a qualification. Similarly, passing a recruitment examination is not necessarily the same as receiving an appointment.

The board must make these transitions explicit.

# 3. Section 00 — Board identity, legend and navigation

This section defines how the user navigates the examination ecosystem.

## 00.1 Main filters

The whiteboard should support filtering by:

- Education level: school, diploma, UG, PG, professional, doctoral.
- Domain: engineering, medicine, commerce, finance, management, law, arts, defence, government, research and others.
- Examination type: entrance, qualifying, competitive recruitment, professional, semester, certification.
- Conducting authority.
- Admission or recruitment destination.
- Qualification required.
- State / national / institution-specific level.
- Current examination cycle.
- Application status or process stage.
- Age / experience / nationality requirements where relevant.
- Physical, medical, practical, interview or portfolio stages.

## 00.2 Examination categories

| Category                        | Meaning                                                | Example                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| School examination              | Assesses school-level learning                         | CBSE Class 12                               |
| Entrance examination            | Helps determine admission eligibility or selection     | JEE Main                                    |
| Advanced selection examination  | Selects among eligible candidates                      | JEE Advanced                                |
| Eligibility test                | Establishes a particular eligibility status            | UGC-NET, where applicable                   |
| Recruitment examination         | Selects candidates for jobs                            | UPSC Civil Services Examination             |
| Professional examination        | Forms part of a professional qualification             | CA examinations                             |
| Aptitude / practical assessment | Measures a specific ability                            | Design aptitude or military selection tests |
| University examination          | Assesses course completion                             | Semester / final examination                |
| Licensing-related assessment    | Forms part of a professional authorisation process     | Applicable professional assessment          |
| Language / international test   | Measures a skill used for admissions or other purposes | IELTS, TOEFL                                |

One examination can belong to more than one operational category. For example, a professional examination can be both a qualification requirement and a step towards membership.

## 00.3 Result and outcome legend

The board should explicitly distinguish these outcomes:

- Registered candidate.
- Eligible candidate.
- Examination attempted.
- Qualified candidate.
- Score obtained.
- Rank obtained.
- Shortlisted candidate.
- Counselling-eligible candidate.
- Seat allotted.
- Admission confirmed.
- Course completed.
- Degree awarded.
- Professional training completed.
- Licence / membership granted.
- Recruitment recommendation.
- Appointment issued.
- Training completed.
- Final career status.

These statuses must not be merged into one generic “passed” node.

| Result date                | Official declaration date                                   |

| Admission/joining deadline | Final confirmation date                                     |
| Source                     | Official notice or schedule                                 |
| Status                     | Confirmed / tentative / not announced / revised / cancelled |

### Critical date rule

The board must never fabricate an examination date or assume that last year's date will repeat.

Use three separate labels:

- Confirmed: published in an applicable official notice.
- Tentative: explicitly identified as provisional by the responsible authority.
- Not announced: no current official date has been verified.

A previous year's date can be shown as historical reference only, clearly separated from the current cycle.

## 02.3 Dependency timeline

The board should also model dependencies. For example:

- Application submission precedes an admit card.
- An entrance result precedes the relevant counselling stage.
- Counselling registration precedes seat allocation where required.
- Document verification may be required before admission or appointment.
- A professional qualification may require practical training after or alongside examinations.
- Joining a defence academy may require additional selection and medical stages.

If a date changes, all dependent nodes should show the updated schedule and source.

## 02.4 Calendar views

The eventual application should provide:

- Annual calendar.
- Monthly calendar.
- Examination-specific timeline.
- Application deadline list.
- Results and counselling calendar.
- Personal milestone checklist.
- Overlapping-exam warnings.
- Source and last-verified date for each event.

These views should be generated from the same underlying examination data, rather than maintaining separate, inconsistent date lists.

## 04.2 JEE Main — National Testing Agency

Purpose: A national entrance examination used for specified engineering admissions and other notified programmes.

### Eligibility and application fields

- Qualifying examination and subject requirements.
- Applicable Class 12 year and passing conditions.
- Minimum marks or other eligibility criteria, if required by the target institution.
- Age-related conditions, if any, under the applicable admission rules.
- Attempt and examination-session rules.
- Application fee and correction window.
- Current official information bulletin.

The eligibility rules for sitting the examination must be distinguished from the eligibility rules for admission to an IIT, NIT, IIIT or another institution.

### Examination stages

- Registration and application.
- Admit card.
- Computer-based examination.
- Result and score.
- Rank or merit processing where applicable.
- Separate eligibility checks for the chosen institution.
- Counselling or institution-specific admission.

### Outcome

A JEE Main score can be used for the admissions and selection processes specified by the relevant institution and current rules. It is not itself a degree, admission offer or universal guarantee of a seat.

## 04.3 JEE Advanced

### Parent node: IIT undergraduate admission

Required child nodes:

1. JEE Main performance and applicable shortlisting.
2. Current eligibility criteria.
3. Registration for JEE Advanced.
4. Examination.
5. Results and rank list.
6. JoSAA counselling and seat allocation.
7. Institute reporting and admission.
8. Programme commencement.

The board must record the current rules for qualifying through JEE Main, the permitted number of attempts, qualifying examination conditions and any applicable category-specific provisions from the relevant official brochure.

### Outcome

A qualifying rank creates eligibility for the applicable seat-allocation process, subject to the current rules and seat availability. Admission is not confirmed until the required counselling and institutional procedures are completed.

## 04.4 JoSAA and CSAB

These nodes must remain separate from JEE Main and JEE Advanced.

JoSAA — Joint Seat Allocation Authority

- Coordinates the relevant joint seat-allocation process for participating institutions.
- Registration and choice filling.
- Seat allocation according to the applicable rules.
- Acceptance, fee payment and reporting procedures.
- Withdrawal or other permitted options.

CSAB — Central Seat Allocation Board

- Conducts specified additional seat-allocation processes, including relevant special rounds where applicable.
- Uses the current year's eligibility and counselling rules.
- Must be shown as a separate route, not an automatic guaranteed fallback.

### Seat-allocation detail fields

## Important implementation boundary

The content architecture is now specified for all three boards, but this does not yet constitute a verified, exhaustive database of every Indian examination and every current examination date.

When the boards are populated, each examination must be verified against its current official notification. Annual dates, eligibility conditions, selection stages and professional rules can change. Historical dates must remain separate from current schedules, and uncertain information must be labelled rather than guessed.

The planned implementation can use the previously selected React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS, React Flow, ELK.js and Lucide icons stack. All three boards should share one entity database and a common identifier system, allowing a user to move from a government authority to an institution, from an institution to its programme, and from that programme to its entrance examination and eventual career pathway.

All three whiteboards are now defined at the content-architecture level. The next stage is to consolidate their shared data model, finalise the node inventory and decide how to divide the information into navigable canvas regions before generating the actual interactive whiteboards.&#x20;
